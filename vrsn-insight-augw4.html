What we did

Last week: creative iteration across every campaign

Several of our original launch assets had been carrying their campaigns since the relaunch and were starting to show it: rising frequency, softening return, the normal lifecycle of a static ad. Rather than wait for that to show up as a drop in the numbers, we shipped a new batch of executions across all five active campaigns: new angles, new formats, and in two cases a completely different approach to the hook.

The clearest example of why this mattered

Our long-running Plantar Fasciitis asset, the one that had anchored that campaign for weeks, returned 0.36 ROAS this week on continued spend. It was fatiguing exactly as expected. The new assets launched into that same campaign during the refresh are the ones now carrying it. See below.

The result

Four new winners from the refresh

Each of these launched during last week's iteration and is already outperforming the account average. Two are worth calling out specifically: the Barefoot execution is the first asset in weeks to clear break-even on that campaign, and the Plantar native-style hook broke a pattern we had seen consistently: top-of-funnel, curiosity-led creative has generally underperformed on this account, and this one did not.

5.33 ROAS "Choose your pair. Four colours. $69." A simple product-and-offer execution for the general PDP campaign. Highest return of any active ad this week. $135 spend · 6 orders · launched 16 Aug
4.30 ROAS "One pair. Nine days." Built for the Travel campaign around a short, real-life stretch of wear rather than the trip itself. Direct, risk-reversal-led close. $87 spend · 4 orders · launched 21 Aug
8.91 ROAS A native, first-person hook for Plantar Fasciitis Story. Curiosity-led top-of-funnel creative has struggled everywhere else on this account; this one is the exception, at a 19.8% click-through rate. $38 spend · 3 orders · launched 17 Aug
1.72 ROAS "Toe spread without the transition." A close product detail shot for Barefoot, naming the exact benefit competitors' barefoot shoes lack. First asset on this campaign to clear break-even in several weeks. $105 spend · 2 orders · launched 16 Aug

Straight talk on Nurse

Three new executions launched into this campaign as part of the refresh, and none have converted in the two days since. That is not enough data to call it a failure. A handful of orders a day is normal variance at this spend level, but we are not counting it as a win yet either. We are giving it through the end of the week before deciding whether to iterate again or fall back to the proven format.
