Creative Performance

All 10 pages: two headline options for the site and gallery, and every ad asset that worked and the ones to rotate out, with a thumbnail and why. All creative is Blitzads static; body copy is constant per angle, so the image and its on-asset headline are the lever.

How to read this

Proven asset = the exact headline from a winning ad, with metrics. Other Option / Suggestion = recommended copy to test. One rule for the next batch: highest CTR is not the best converter, several high-CTR assets did not convert, so pick on purchases and ROAS.

Asset names match Meta exactly (drop the "Blitzads - VRSN - Fresh Concepts -" prefix). "Low spend" tags mark variants with too little budget to trust the ROAS.
